# Execution Doctrine

Use this module after selecting the primary decision mode and current stage.

## Principles

### Investigation before escalation

Gather only evidence capable of changing the decision, stage, commitment, owner, or timing. Do not use research as indefinite reassurance.

### Principal uncertainty and principal constraint

Identify:

- the uncertainty the next milestone must resolve;
- the constraint currently limiting success, such as data, mechanism, customer pain, student autonomy, capability, distribution, governance, or cognitive capital.

### Stop comparison

When the evidence threshold is met and downside is bounded, stop reopening alternatives until the review trigger. Record foregone alternatives rather than continuing daily comparison.

### Staged commitment

Match resource exposure to evidence:

- investigate to clarify uncertainty;
- validate through a bounded option;
- build capability and repeatability;
- scale only after operational and value evidence;
- reallocate when evidence changes.

### Resource floor and cap

Specify both:

- the minimum commitment needed for a fair test;
- the maximum exposure allowed before review.

An option with no cap becomes an obligation. A core bet receiving only leftover time is not a real commitment.

### First concrete action

Translate abstract ambition into the next observable action. Prefer a draft, call, experiment, coding sample, customer interview, data check, submission, or calendar block over more conceptual planning.

### Low-urgency persistence

Create execution before a crisis forces concentration. Use protected time, short milestone cycles, visible accountability, and a no-reconsideration period.

### Concentration of cognitive capital

When the mode is Own or Build, allocate protected high-quality attention. When Service-track or Delegate, cap attention and define boundaries.

### Bounded responsibility

Clarify what the user owns and does not own. Avoid undefined rescue roles.

### Explicit milestone

A milestone must be observable, time-bounded, and capable of changing the decision. Effort, intention, or activity alone is not a milestone.

### Review and response rule

Pre-commit to the response if the milestone succeeds, partially succeeds, or fails. Avoid automatic extensions.

## Role options

- Problem architect.
- Theory framer.
- Platform owner.
- Supervisor.
- Bounded adviser.
- Co-author.
- Editor or reviewer.
- Data or method infrastructure builder.
- Commercial product owner.
- Executive education designer.
- Venture screener.
- Innovation portfolio architect.

## Execution output

Specify:

1. primary mode and stage;
2. principal uncertainty and constraint;
3. resource floor and cap;
4. protected attention and owner;
5. first concrete action;
6. milestone and evidence standard;
7. persistence period and no-reconsideration rule;
8. review date or condition;
9. action after success, partial success, or failure;
10. foregone alternatives suspended during the period.
