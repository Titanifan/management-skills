## Portfolio interpretation

Prioritise pathways rather than logos.

- **Priority development:** direct senior access, persistent problem, sponsor, reciprocal resources, and credible ownership.
- **Gateway development:** high-quality broker or policy node able to make named introductions.
- **One-shot conversion test:** some promise but missing sponsor, ownership, or resource evidence.
- **Teaching/service portfolio:** valuable WIL, education, duty, or goodwill with no research conversion claim.
- **Dormant/reactivation:** prior value exists but contact or sponsor has lapsed.
- **Archive:** little evidence across the five-question dashboard and no plausible trigger for change.

Do not rank a relationship highly because the organisation is famous, wealthy, or has collaborated for many semesters. Rank it by the quality of the pathway to decision authority, reciprocal resources, attributable ownership, and durable outputs.
