# Adaptive Commitment Handoff

Use this file only after Strategic Opportunity has classified an opportunity as a core candidate, selective candidate, service-only opportunity requiring deliberate handling, or a Zero-to-One opportunity worth further consideration.

Do not recreate Adaptive Commitment inside this skill.

## Strategic Opportunity decides

- whether the opportunity deserves portfolio entry or further consideration;
- platform classification: platform-core, platform-adjacent, or legacy/opportunistic;
- portfolio disposition: core candidate, selective candidate, service-only, monitor, redesign, or reject;
- dual-track class when relevant: Build-to-Own Candidate, Impact Practice, Venture Hypothesis, or Redesign/Reject;
- principal uncertainty, binding strategic weakness, and evidence gap;
- expected compounding and residual assets;
- role, control, ownership, and governance constraints.

## Adaptive Commitment decides

- execution mode: Own, Build, Option, Service-track, Delegate, Park, Kill, or Commercialise;
- current stage: Investigate, Validate, Build, Scale, or Reallocate;
- resource floor and cap;
- protected attention and responsible owner;
- first action and milestone;
- evidence threshold;
- persistence period;
- review condition;
- scale, redesign, delegate, park, kill, and reallocation rules.

## Minimal handoff record

Transfer:

1. **Opportunity definition:** the precise opportunity selected for consideration.
2. **Platform classification:** platform-core, platform-adjacent, or legacy/opportunistic.
3. **Portfolio disposition:** core candidate, selective candidate, service-only, monitor, redesign, or reject.
4. **Zero-to-One class:** when applicable.
5. **Current evidence stage:** idea, problem evidence, prototype, pilot, repeated use, paid use, workflow integration, or scale evidence.
6. **Principal uncertainty:** the one unknown most likely to change the strategic verdict.
7. **Binding constraint:** capability, data, access, team, governance, distribution, ownership, time, or capital.
8. **Positive evidence:** what would strengthen the opportunity case.
9. **Negative evidence:** what would weaken or invalidate the opportunity case.
10. **Residual assets:** useful theory, data, method, relationship, distribution, capability, or insight that could remain.
11. **Governance boundary:** material role, ownership, conflict, confidentiality, or institutional constraints.

Stop once this transfer is complete. Adaptive Commitment owns the execution design.
